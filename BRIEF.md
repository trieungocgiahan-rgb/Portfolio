# The brief (keep this. it is the reason the site looks like this)

## Role to work in
Act as a creative director, editorial storyteller, interaction designer,
information architect, typography specialist and a frontend engineer
obsessed with craft. Not as a developer patching CSS section by section.

## What this is
The experience of meeting a person through the web. An interactive
editorial story, an exhibition, a personal archive. Not a digital résumé.

## The person
Triệu Ngọc Gia Hân, English name Katherine Trieu, Grade 12, Trưng Vương
High School, Ho Chi Minh City. Works in Vietnamese and English. Interested
in cognitive science, psychology, HCI, human behaviour, art and community
building. Those labels are not the story.

## The narrative thesis
I notice what changes a room. A room can be a dinner table, a workshop, a
concert, a shelter, an interface, a research session, a classroom, a
conversation with an AI. The recurring movement is from separate to shared.
Individual crayons to one shared tray. Strangers to an audience. An emotion
to something visible. A question to an experiment.

Let the visitor infer this. Never announce it as a personal-brand statement.

## Forbidden structure
Hero, About Me, Skills, Projects grid, Awards, Timeline, Contact.
No endless rectangular project cards. No skill pills or percentage meters.
No "Hi, I'm X 👋" followed by "passionate about creating meaningful
experiences". No generic Bento grids, gradient blobs, glassmorphism,
mouse-following orbs, or "Selected Work" followed by three identical cards.
If an element could appear on 500 other student portfolios, redesign it.

## Chapter architecture
01 Hello, the short story and the observation
02 Rooms, Nét Mơ and where the noticing started
03 Questions, what makes someone participate, the crayon tray
04 Questions continued, the study, plus questions carried into other rooms
05 Building, Contextuary and The Colorful Journey
06 Gather, concerts, leagues, festivals, louder and faster
07 Pattern, different rooms and the same question, the quietest section
08 Open, looking forward, the door stays open

Deep spaces behind Doors: Nét Mơ, Contextuary, The Colorful Journey,
the research, Gather, Exhibition, About, CV.

## Door motif
Knock entrance, thresholds, reveals, rooms, openings. Felt, not explained.
No cartoon doors, no "welcome to my world". The main story must stay
scrollable start to finish without ever choosing between doors.

## Visual direction
Editorial, human, curious, intelligent, warm, slightly experimental,
art-directed, precise, alive. Somewhere between an independent magazine,
a small contemporary exhibition, an experimental design studio, a personal
notebook and a well-built digital product.
Not corporate, not tech-bro, not luxury-fashion clone, not cyberpunk,
not a template, not childish, not empty minimalism.

Warm cream editorial spaces, deep navy exhibition spaces, accents of pink,
orange, teal, blue, violet and coral. Colour is narrative punctuation.

## Typography
Treat it as architecture. One expressive display moment only, in the hero.
Everything else restrained. No giant philosophical statement opening every
section. Prevent widows and one-word final lines. Vietnamese must render
perfectly, so never hyphenate and never break a word mid-syllable.

## Images
Photography is central and must feel curated. Four roles, used
deliberately. Hero, evidence (numbered, archival caption), artifact (small,
object-like, slightly rotated), gallery (artwork with breathing room).
Never identical rounded rectangles in an even grid.

## Motion
Every interaction earns its place by revealing meaning. No fade-up on
everything. Use uncovering, masking, sticky evidence, convergence, drift,
counters only where a number is the evidence. The page must still feel
deliberate with all animation disabled.

## Copywriting
Specific observations, questions, objects, moments, behaviour, small
details, short reflections. No polished personal-brand language. Do not
explain every metaphor. Demonstrate character through what was noticed and
made, never by naming traits.

## The main page stays lean
Each chapter on the main scroll is one short line of text, one visual (a
photograph, a number, an interaction) and one way in ("Read more", an event
link). Long paragraphs, lists and Vietnamese session names live in the deep
spaces. Right after Hello, a map of five arched doors shows the whole story
at a glance: one door per chapter, each with its project, role and one
number. On a phone the first thing seen is "Hi, I'm Gia Hân."

## Three depths
30 seconds, the visitor feels the character and intellectual energy.
2 to 4 minutes, they understand how community work, research, design and
technology relate. An admissions officer can still reach responsibilities,
metrics, methodology, dates and the CV without polluting the narrative.

## Quality test for every section
Could this appear unchanged on another student's portfolio, does it reveal
how she thinks, does the animation reveal meaning, would it still feel
deliberate with motion off, does mobile feel authored rather than merely
responsive, is any text clipped or crowded at iPad width.
