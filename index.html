04Study

Asking it properly.

If a feeling comes out easier in the right room, what happens when the room is a conversation with a machine?

Two Doors Into the Same Room · Lead author · N = 68 · 2025–26

Method and findings

What came back

The lines cross: neither door won. It depends on who walks in.

Read the finding

People with little art experience got further through dialogue, where the structure is handed to them. People already fluent with materials got further by making. Averaging the two conditions would have hidden the whole result.

The seven

Seven participants left the AI condition in a worse mood than they entered it. They are in the paper, with their reflections.
