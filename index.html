04Questions

Asking it properly.

If a feeling comes out easier in the right room, what happens when the room is a conversation with a machine?

Art

45 min · materials

↔
AI

45 min · dialogue

Two Doors Into the Same Room · N = 68 · Nov 2025 to Feb 2026 · SPSS · advised by Nguyễn Phương Thảo, Bảo Sang Psychology Space · prepared for NHSJS

Method and limitations

Design

Two conditions, matched session length, art familiarity recorded before assignment. Every AI session ran on the same model version, so the room was identical for everyone.

What came back

Schematic, not the raw plot. The lines cross: neither door won.

Read the finding

People with little art experience got further through dialogue, where the structure is handed to them. People already fluent with materials got further by making, where structure gets in the way. Averaging the two conditions would have hidden the whole result.

The seven

Seven participants left the AI condition in a worse mood than they entered it. They are in the paper, with their reflections. The common thread: a good question asked at a moment when there was no way to leave the conversation.

Questions I carried into other rooms

What does attention look like when you can actually watch it?

  • Trained in brain mapping, não đồ / EEG
  • Filtering and cleaning recorded data
  • Running surveys with student participants

Brainlife · 10-month internship

Cleaning other people's data taught me how much of a finding is decided before analysis starts: which segments get dropped, which participant counts as usable. I write my own method sections differently now.

What do teenagers actually say when someone asks properly?

  • 56 in-depth interviews, synthesised into one persona
  • Designed the core product: a storytelling e-book where teens become a chef
  • 5 themed storytelling surveys · outreach through a character account

Unwrapped · paid R&D team · 2024–25

Nobody described their feelings when asked directly. They described them fluently while talking about a character doing something with their hands. That is the same finding the study later tested.

Does a room of 600 behave like a room of 20?

  • Team of 8 rewriting psychology research for students
  • Self-discovery festival reaching 600 students
  • Peer support around exam season, 200+ reached

EUNOIA · Head of Academic Affairs

Scale does not dilute participation, structure does. The festival worked because 600 people were given something small to do in the first two minutes.
