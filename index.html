01Hello

I keep noticing the minute a group of people stops being separate.

It is always smaller than you would expect. A tray pushed to the middle. One sheet of paper instead of six.

I have been drawing since I was five. The questions I could not answer from inside an art workshop turned into a study, a tool and some very loud nights.
