04Asking it properly

If a feeling comes out easier in the right room, what happens when the room is a conversation with a machine?

Two Doors Into the Same Room · N = 68 · Nov 2025 to Feb 2026 · SPSS · advised by Nguyễn Phương Thảo, Bảo Sang Psychology Space · prepared for NHSJS

Method and limitations

Design

Two conditions, matched session length, art familiarity recorded before assignment. Every AI session ran on the same model version, so the room was identical for everyone.

What came back

Schematic, not the raw plot. The lines cross: neither door won.

Read the finding

People with little art experience got further through dialogue, where the structure is handed to them. People already fluent with materials got further by making, where structure gets in the way. Averaging the two conditions would have hidden the whole result.

The seven

Seven participants left the AI condition in a worse mood than they entered it. They are in the paper, with their reflections. The common thread: a good question asked at a moment when there was no way to leave the conversation.
